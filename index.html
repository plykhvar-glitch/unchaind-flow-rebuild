What this is

We went through Unchaind the way a new user does, then rebuilt the paywall where we think money is left on the table.

Adapty powers subscriptions for 30,000+ apps, so we get to see what moves conversion and ARPU across the market rather than inside one app. We pointed that at Unchaind: walked your onboarding to the paywall, closed it to see what happens next, read your App Store listing and its in-app purchase list, and looked for gaps between what you already have and what the paywall shows.

This time we left your onboarding alone and looked only at the paywall and the moment someone closes it. Five ideas, not fifteen. Each one is a test you can run, and each is built into a clickable prototype. The prototype uses your design, not ours.

We did not redesign your app

Your dark paywall, serif headlines, purple button, the three plan tiles with their Popular and Best offer badges, the feature list, reviews, community posts, comparison table and FAQ are all still there. A few things moved, and a few things you already own now show up at the right moment.

You may have tested some of this

We are working from the outside, so we cannot see your experiment history. If one of these is something you have already run, tell us and we will go deeper on the rest rather than repeat it.
