# Growth Audit — Unchaind

**Live:** https://plykhvar-glitch.github.io/unchaind-flow-rebuild/

Five ideas for the Unchaind: Overcome Lust paywall, each built into a clickable prototype in Unchaind's own design: the dark paywall, serif headlines, purple button, plan tiles and badges. This audit covers the paywall and what happens when someone closes it. The onboarding is left as it ships.

Open on a phone: https://plykhvar-glitch.github.io/unchaind-flow-rebuild/?open=proto

## The five ideas

| # | Idea | What we saw | Potential outcome |
| :-- | :-- | :-- | :-- |
| 01 | Give people who close it one more option | Closing the paywall returns to the plan screen. The PRO Yearly at $49.99 in your store, less than two quarters, never appears. | ARPU +10–15% |
| 02 | Put their quit date in the headline | The plan screen promises a quit date 90 days out; the paywall headline drops it. | CR +15–20% |
| 03 | Show your 4.8 and your press above the plans | 4.8 from 41K ratings, “#1 App for Christians” and four press logos live in your App Store screenshots, not on the paywall. | CR +10–15% |
| 04 | Let the plan maths replace the 87% timer | Quarterly at $39.99 is 62% less than 13 weeks at $7.99 and covers the 90-day plan exactly. | ARPU +10–15% |
| 05 | Answer “will anyone know?” at the button | Privacy is fourth in the FAQ; reviews mention cancelling. One line above the button answers both. | CR +3–8% |

The prototype has light and dark modes, English, French and Spanish, and a 30-second walkthrough.

## Grounding

Every price, rating, quote, logo and feature line comes from Unchaind's own App Store listing (including its in-app purchase list) and its in-app screens. Impact ranges are Adapty's expected effect from teardowns and A/B tests across subscription apps, not measured lift for Unchaind.
